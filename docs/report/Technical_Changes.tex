\section*{1. Actual grillage stiffness and the deflection adjustment}%
\label{sec:1.Actualgrillagestiffnessandthedeflectionadjustment}%
The generated OpenSees elasticBeamColumn commands for physical girders G1, G2 and G3 assign A = 0.02388 m2, E = 2.000e11 Pa, G = 7.692e10 Pa and Iz = 0.008328 m4. The saved input DTO before command formatting carries Iz = 0.0083277576 m4. Command values were inspected after building the actual 25 m Ujjain bridge.%


%
These longitudinal elements carry steel{-}section stiffness. The final transformed composite inertia 0.022207031145 m4 is not assigned to them. There is therefore no direct second application of the same composite longitudinal inertia. Transverse members are the existing deck{-}grid proxies and are also paired with the steel material.%


%
The designer uses final design{-}section Isteel = 8770693002.666668 mm4 and Icomp = 22207031145.0 mm4, giving r = 2.5319585508520372. It divides the raw displacement by this ratio. The model uses the earlier input{-}stage steel inertia, which differs from the final design inertia. The scalar adjustment is consequently a post{-}processing approximation rather than an exact composite{-}grillage reanalysis.%


%
The governing live check is G2 under Case2 1xClass70R. Raw Dy is about 35.360 mm and adjusted Dy is about 13.966 mm, recorded as 13.97 mm against 31.25 mm. The plotted 1.0 LL case is a separate case selected by bending moment and has a physical{-}girder raw maximum around 32.146 mm. Dynamic allowance is already included by the vehicle generator.%


%
After the patch integration correction, the governing adjusted total displacement is recorded as 24.54 mm against 41.67 mm. The report prints the actual per{-}case inertia and displacement trace. Existing PASS statuses for these adjusted checks are retained. Coupled{-}grid, staged{-}construction and creep behavior are not validated by this scalar arithmetic, and the report no longer claims long{-}term composite stiffness was used for this adjustment.%

\section*{2. Independent load{-}factor and bridge benchmarks}%
\label{sec:2.Independentload{-}factorandbridgebenchmarks}%
Every relevant distribution path was exercised with independently known resultants: a 10 kN point load, a 10 kN/m line over 25 m, a 1 kN/m2 patch over 25 by 6 m, and a compound point{-}plus{-}patch load. Factors {-}1.0, 0.5 and 1.5 produce the corresponding signed integrated forces. Nodal force and moment scaling, nested group coefficients and outer load{-}case coefficients are also tested.%


%
A separate simply supported 25 m, three{-}girder, zero{-}skew bridge uses 21 transverse stations and a symmetric 10 kN/m tributary load on each girder. The independently expected midspan moment is w L2 / 8 = 781.250 kNm. OSPGrillage gives approximately 781.296 kNm, within the 0.1 percent regression tolerance.%


%
Factoring by 1.35 gives a theoretical 1054.6875 kNm and a computed approximately 1054.749 kNm. Applying 1.35 as a group coefficient or as the outer case coefficient gives matching responses. Each support's directly queried vertical reaction is 506.25 kN, matching 3 times 10 times 25 divided by 2 times 1.35.%


%
This benchmark checks moment, equilibrium and scaling independently. It does not use the production report's post{-}adjusted deflection as a reference solution.%

\section*{3. Confirmed patch{-}load corrections}%
\label{sec:3.Confirmedpatch{-}loadcorrections}%
A negative patch pressure exposed a centroid bug: get\_patch\_centroid replaced every non{-}positive total weight with 1. Negative uniform pressure therefore moved the computed centroid outside the mesh and the patch could disappear. The calculation now divides by the signed total and uses the geometric vertex mean when the total is zero.%


%
A full{-}grid patch also counted complete boundary cells twice: once in the fully bounded{-}cell path and again in the intersection path. The intersection pass now skips cells already integrated completely.%


%
Before the boundary correction, a 150 kN full{-}width patch could distribute 165 kN. After both corrections its signed resultant is exactly 150 kN before factoring. Partial patches spanning grid boundaries and patches aligned to grid lines reproduce their independent area integrals. The fixes operate on load integration and do not alter results to obtain passing checks.%

\section*{4. Fatigue, shear buckling and report consistency}%
\label{sec:4.Fatigue,shearbucklingandreportconsistency}%
Independent extraction from the regenerated xarray force dataset gives G3 frequent{-}SLS Mz extrema of {-}1541780.200848 and +1541779.296757 Nm. The implemented signed{-}local{-}moment range is 3083559.497605 Nm. With composite section modulus 16982734.444305 mm3, the stress is 181.570259 MPa. Dividing by the displayed fatigue capacity 92.49 MPa gives UR 1.963134, consistent with the recorded 1.9631 and displayed 1.96.%


%
Independent ULS extraction gives G1 vertical shear 488.729602 kN. Dividing by shear{-}buckling capacity 443.02 kN gives UR 1.103177, consistent with recorded 1.1032 and displayed 1.10. G3 also fails shear buckling at about 1.0696. Intermediate{-}stiffener failures remain present.%


%
The fatigue number is verified as the output of the implemented signed local end{-}force envelope. Its interpretation as the physical cyclic stress range remains unconfirmed because i{-}end and j{-}end sign conventions and cross{-}case extrema require an engineering fatigue{-}range definition. The report preserves the failure and states this limitation rather than silently changing the calculation.%


%
Executive summary, individual fatigue/shear checks, overall summary and component chart were compared with the recorded governing results. Component maximum ratios are approximately 1.9631 for plate girders, 0.9483 for deck slab, 0.54664 for cross bracing and 0.97 for diaphragms. The aggregate fatigue row is labelled as a signed frequent{-}SLS force envelope.%

\section*{5. Analysed actions and incomplete coverage}%
\label{sec:5.Analysedactionsandincompletecoverage}%
Combination construction now records actions requested by selected combinations but unavailable in the model. In this run they are vehicle\_collision, barge\_impact, floating\_bodies and thermal\_load. Actual combination expressions still include only available applied actions.%


%
Executive{-}summary load coverage is explicitly Incomplete. If no assessed check fails but requested actions are unavailable or coverage is unknown, the overall status cannot become Pass. A valid failure continues to determine the overall Fail status. Applicability of unavailable event actions must be established separately.%


%
Member{-}end vertical force sums remain distinguished from direct nodal support reactions. The benchmark queries actual nodal reactions, while the production dataset still stores member forces without a direct reaction array. Reinforcement quantities still require a complete schedule.%
